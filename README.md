# OHRR App

An application supporting **Ohio House Rabbit Rescue (OHRR)** — a Columbus, Ohio 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated exclusively to domestic (pet) rabbits — across everything they do: **rescue, adoption, education, and their flagship annual fundraiser, [Midwest BunFest](https://www.midwestbunfest.org/).**

The goal is to collapse OHRR's scattered, weekend-bound operations (a website, Facebook, Petfinder/Adoptapet, an Amazon wishlist, Kroger rewards, multiple donation pages, and a separate BunFest site) into **one always-on hub** — so their reach isn't capped by two 4-hour windows a week and a handful of volunteers' spare time.

## What the app supports

- **Rescue & adoption** — get more rabbits adopted into qualified homes, faster.
- **Education** — teach good rabbit care so fewer rabbits are surrendered.
- **Fundraising** — grow and diversify revenue, anchored by Midwest BunFest.
- **Volunteer & foster capacity** — make it easy for people to give time.

## Recommended first build: the Midwest BunFest companion

Midwest BunFest is the largest rabbit-centered expo in the Eastern US, held every October, with a national/international draw and a built-in network of 15–20 Midwest rescue partners that already promote it. It's the one moment each year when thousands of exactly-the-right people are paying attention.

The recommended first move is to **build the BunFest companion** — session schedule, event map, vendor directory, silent auction & raffle, rescue-partner directory, sponsor recognition, registration, and the Rabbit Attendance Agreement. Use it to capture the once-a-year national audience into the app, then retain them with **year-round giving + education**. That turns OHRR's existing biggest asset into the engine for everything else.
