## 1. Who they are (at a glance)

Ohio House Rabbit Rescue is a Columbus, Ohio nonprofit dedicated **exclusively to domestic (pet) rabbits**. They run an adoption center, rescue rabbits in need, and educate the public on keeping rabbits as indoor companions. They operate what they describe as the **largest rescue-and-adoption facility exclusively for domestic rabbits in Ohio** — and the first of its kind in the state.

### Origin story
- **Founded 2009** by Beverly May, a longtime rabbit owner.
- The catalyst: roughly **900+ rabbits were being surrendered each year in Central Ohio alone**, with no dedicated rabbit rescue/adoption center to take them in.
- **2013:** opened the **Ohio House Rabbit Adoption Center** — the first rescue-and-adoption center in Ohio exclusively for rabbits.

### Mission (as they state it)
> Operate an Adoption Center for rescued pet rabbits in need, offer a robust adoption program, and provide educational resources and programming on the proper care of rabbits as indoor companions.

A stated operational goal is to grow adoptions to **125+ per year**.

## 3. What they do — programs & services

### Adoption
- Core program. Rabbits are **spayed/neutered and vaccinated prior to adoption**.
- **Adoption requirements** for the home: rabbit must live **indoors**, in a minimum **4×4 space or free-range** inside the home; fed **high-quality pellets, unlimited hay, and a daily salad**.
- Adoptions are **by appointment**.

### Fostering
- Team members and volunteers foster rabbits in their homes as needed to expand capacity beyond the adoption center.

### Volunteering (hands-on and behind-the-scenes)
- **Bunny socialization** — volunteers sit with rabbits to prepare them for adoption (currently limited to **no more than two 1-hour shifts per month**).
- **Vet transportation** — driving rabbits to/from vet appointments.
- **Awareness** — social media and event support.
- They also reference a **"Volunteer Family."**

### Education & rabbit-care resources
- Online **Bunny Care Resources** covering spay/neuter, **bonding bunnies**, **diet**, and **litter-box training**, among other topics. (https://www.ohiohouserabbitrescue.org/rabbit-care/resources/)
- Public education on rabbits as indoor companions is a central pillar of the mission, not an afterthought.

### Fix-a-Bun (FAB) — low-cost spay/neuter
- Launched **2012**. Provides financial assistance for spay/neuter surgery for rabbits across Ohio (not just OHRR's own animals).

### Admissions / owner surrender
- They accept **owner surrenders / relinquishments** via a form, addressing the large local surrender volume that prompted the org's founding.

### Hop Shop (merchandise)
- A merchandise shop at the Adoption Center (and a presence at BunFest) — both a revenue stream and a way to get rabbit-appropriate supplies into owners' hands.

## 5. Midwest BunFest — the flagship event

**Midwest BunFest** is OHRR's marquee annual fundraiser and the largest rabbit-centered festival and educational exposition in the **Eastern United States**. It draws national and international attendees — pet owners, rabbit experts, and veterinarians — and is promoted by **15–20 rabbit rescue organizations** from around the Midwest.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cadence** | Annual, every **October** (fall) |
| **2024 edition** | Sunday, **Oct 27, 2024**, 10:00 am – 4:00 pm |
| **Location (current)** | Hilliard, OH area |
| **Earlier location** | 2012 edition held at the Whitehall Community Center, Columbus |
| **Site** | https://www.midwestbunfest.org/ |
| **Facebook** | https://www.facebook.com/MidwestBunFest/ |

### What happens at BunFest
- **Vendors / Bun Specialty Shopping** — supplies you can't find in local pet stores: hard-to-find toys, handcrafted items, homemade treats.
- **Educational programs** — **10–15 sessions** led by nationally recognized veterinarians and certified rabbit educators.
- **Bunny Spa** — nail trims and grooming.
- **Bunny Glamour Shots** — pet rabbit photography.
- **Silent Auction** and **Raffle**.
- **OHRR Hop Shop**.
- **Rescue Partners** — meet rabbit rescues from across the Midwest.
- **Chilaxabun Lounge** — chill/relax space.
- **Sponsors** at multiple levels.
- Attendees may **bring their own rabbits** (subject to a Rabbit Attendance Agreement).

### On the "15+ years" history
The project sponsor noted BunFest has been running for **over 15 years**. OHRR itself was **founded in 2009**, consistent with a roughly 15-year-old event. ## 6. Ecosystem & related organizations (don't confuse these)

Several rabbit organizations operate in the same region — useful context, and worth keeping distinct in any app data model:

- **Ohio House Rabbit Rescue (OHRR)** — our client. Columbus; founded 2009.
- **Columbus House Rabbit Society** (columbusrabbit.org) — produces a well-regarded downloadable Rabbit Care & Behavior Booklet; OHRR points to it as a resource.
- **Buckeye House Rabbit Society** (ohare.org) — Ohio rabbit org active since 1997.
- **House Rabbit Society** (houserabbit.org) — national umbrella org / humane education.

The **Midwest rescue partners** (15–20 orgs that promote and appear at BunFest) are a built-in network worth understanding when we think about app reach.
